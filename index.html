Dispatch Link

Lead CRM and dispatch platform for trucking companies

The problem

Dispatch companies kept leads in spreadsheets, made calls from personal phones and tracked loads in a separate system. Managers had no clear view of who was doing what.

What I built

One platform where sales agents process and call leads, dispatchers manage carriers and loads, and owners monitor the whole team, each with their own login and access.

Key features

  • Lead scanning by MC range
  • One-click calling
  • Automatic follow-ups
  • Email proposals
  • Loads and invoices
  • Calling leaderboard
  • Shift reports
  • Company licences

Hotel POS

Point-of-sale system for hotels and restaurants

The problem

Restaurants taking orders on paper lost track of tables, sent unclear tickets to the kitchen and had to add up the day's sales by hand.

What I built

A browser-based POS that handles the full flow from table to kitchen to bill, with reports at the end of the day. Nothing to install, and it works on any device.

Key features

  • Table management
  • Kitchen screen
  • Menu with categories
  • Sold-out switch
  • Orders and bills
  • Reservations
  • Expenses
  • Sales reports

FAQ

Questions clients often ask

Have a different question? Ask me on WhatsApp.

Do I need any technical knowledge?

No. You explain how your work is done today, and I handle the technical side. When it's ready, I show you how to use it in plain language.

Will I have to change the tools I use?

Usually not. I connect the automation to what you already use, like WhatsApp, Gmail, Google Sheets or your CRM, so your team keeps working the way it does now.

What if the AI gets something wrong?

I test every automation on your real cases before launch. For sensitive steps, like sending a quote or a payment reminder, I add an approval step so a person checks it first.

Is my business data safe?

I use official APIs, give the automation access only to what it needs, and set everything up in your own accounts. You keep full ownership and control.

How long does a project take, and what does it cost?

A simple automation can be ready in a few days. Larger systems take a few weeks. The price depends on scope, and you always get a fixed quote before any work starts.

What happens after launch?

Fixes are free for 30 days. After that, you can choose a monthly support plan, or contact me whenever you want changes.
